Email, domain privacy and other separate charges

The three-month email trial

The selected one-mailbox Professional Email trial renews at $$19.99/year after three months. It is a separate cart item, not a rise in the prepaid hosting price. Other mailbox counts were not priced here. The terms say trials become paid subscriptions unless canceled before the trial ends; verify the email subscription's own renewal setting and date in your panel.

A .COM renewal can be charged before the expiry date

DreamHost's domain Auto-Renew instructions say a credit card is charged 31 days before expiration for applicable extensions, including .COM. They also say the Auto-Renew status cannot be changed within that 31-day renewal period. Domain renewals are nonrefundable. Check the domain setting separately from hosting cancellation.

Paraphrase of official domain Auto-Renew billing instructions, read $auto_read. This is a domain rule, not a 31-day hosting cancellation deadline.

How do you cancel, and when does the site stop?

You can cancel by accessing the Manage Account panel page.

Official Terms of Service, read $terms_read.

The account-closure guide describes an online Manage Account flow: back up your data, review the products and domains to retain, check balances, and confirm closure. No telephone step is listed. Closing an account and removing one hosting plan are different actions; review exactly which services the confirmation covers.

Instructions read $closure_read. We read the process; we did not cancel an account.

Official timing conflict: the general terms describe termination at the end of the current term. The shared-hosting deletion guide, updated October 6, says the shared plan ends immediately on cancellation, while another part describes a pending-cancellation state. It covers shared plans with another active service. We cannot establish which effective date a particular four-year account would show. Back up first and verify the date displayed in your account before confirming.

Terms read $terms_read; shared-plan instructions read $cancel_read.

Cancellation is not automatically a full refund

The plan advertises a 30-day money-back guarantee. The terms limit the Website Hosting guarantee to eligible credit-card or PayPal purchases and exclude items such as email, domains and SSL certificates. The refund guide distinguishes account credit from a refund; we did not establish a four-year renewal refund entitlement. Do not assume unused prepaid years will be refunded.

If you used the free-domain credit, the account-closure instructions describe a registration charge on closure, with an exception near domain expiry. That credit was not used in our cart.

Refund and credit guide, read $refund_read; terms read $terms_read; closure instructions read $closure_read. No refund or cancellation tested.
