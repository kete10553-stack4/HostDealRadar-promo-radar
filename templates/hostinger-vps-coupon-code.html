How to find and check the VPS code

  1. Open Hostinger's official coupon page. Under Pick your perfect plan, select VPS hosting. The default web-hosting cards describe different products.
  2. Find your KVM plan and check that its card still displays COUPONSPAGE as applied. Choose that card's Use coupon link.
  3. Before paying, check the selected product, term, applied code, currency, tax, and total due in your own order. If the code is absent or rejected, do not treat this dated page observation as proof that your order received it.

What the official cards establish

All four named VPS cards showed the same code during this check. They are four product observations of one code, not four separate coupons. The saved observations identify the selected tab, capture time, and wording beside each plan.

Hostinger limits coupons to one per order. Its official VPS pricing page says payment is upfront and the displayed monthly rate is the term's total divided by its months. Read the billing terms on that page and your selected order; this check does not infer an initial commitment or total from a monthly figure.
