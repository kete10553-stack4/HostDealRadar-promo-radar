Cloudways’ site also advertises other products and offers; this guide does not treat those as a replacement hosting coupon code.

What the expired offer said about eligibility and later billing

Cloudways said the Summer offer was for new users who upgraded from a free trial to a paid plan. Its FAQ said only one discount code could be used at checkout. It also said discounts typically applied to the initial promotional period, after which standard pricing applied. Those terms describe the ended Summer offer; they do not establish a current discount or a renewal price.

Before you start a paid plan

  1. Check Cloudways’ official promo and pricing pages for a newly dated offer. Do not assume the expired Summer code will apply.
  2. Check your account’s eligibility and the actual discount displayed during checkout.
  3. Review the plan, cloud provider, usage charges, add-ons, tax, and the total before paying.
  4. Confirm what standard pricing applies after any introductory period. A promotional rate is not a renewal-price guarantee.
