Archived Cloudways offers

These are historical records, not current deals, and the six-snapshot table with each year’s code and cited snapshot now sits in the Cloudways coupon archive; the short answers that follow stay on this page.

Can you identify a Cloudways offer from the code alone?

No. The 2018 Cloudways roundup also records an Inspectlet offer using BFCM18. The 2019 roundup records a NotificationX offer using BFCM40 and a MexBS offer using cloudways-BFCM. Those entries describe the partners’ products, not additional Cloudways hosting discounts. The product and issuer beside a code determine what it was for.

Could an existing customer use BFCM4030?

The archived page does not give one clean answer. Its terms say the offer is “only valid for new customers.” On the same page, an FAQ says it applies to users who sign up and upgrade from trial to a full account. We report both statements because the source contradicts itself. Neither statement proves that an existing paid account qualified. See the cited December 2022 snapshot.

Could two coupons be stacked?

The December 2022 terms allow one coupon per claimant, and the FAQ says coupons are limited to one use per order. That is a historical rule from this saved page. It is not a current account or checkout test. See the cited December 2022 snapshot.

Why do the countdown and coupon counter not prove availability?

The 2021 and both 2022 responses retain the same initial countdown digits and 2021 date values in their HTML. That establishes what the stored responses contain. It does not tell us how JavaScript behaved in a visitor’s browser or how much time an offer had left. The current Summer-page countdown is discussed separately above.

The December 2022 response also contains a coupon counter’s initial HTML value beside a total-coupon label. Those values do not establish how many coupons remained redeemable.

The same stored responses contain hidden date values from October and November 2021, including in the 2022 snapshots. We treat them as initial HTML values, not as verified 2022 campaign dates.

Limits of the comparison

Retained notes from September 21, 2026 record nine readable result pages and one Reddit result that could not be read because of a network-security block. DevOpsCube already listed BFCM4030 and SUMMER306 as expired. This was a limited, dated comparison, not a claim that these findings are unique across the web. The unread result is not used as evidence of absence.

Before you start a paid plan

1. Check the current official pages. Open the official promo and pricing pages, then confirm the actual total and any discount at checkout.

2. Check eligibility. Confirm that your account qualifies for the offer shown in the order flow.

3. Check the whole bill. Review the plan, cloud provider, usage charges, add-ons, tax, and total before paying.

4. Keep historical evidence in the past. Do not infer a renewal price or a current promotion from archived page material.

Scope: this guide records the cited official source response only. It does not establish that no account-specific or separately advertised offer exists, or that any offer will apply to every checkout.

What were Cloudways’ own Black Friday codes?

Its own pages printed BFCM18 with 30% for 3 months in 2018, BFCM40 with 40% for 3 months and a December 4, 2019 deadline in 2019, BFCM2021 with 40% for 4 months plus an advertised $10 hosting credit in 2021, and BFCM4030 with 40% for 4 months plus up to 30 free migrations in 2022.
