How to check the offer before paying

1. Open the official coupon page. Choose the exact web-hosting plan and read its upfront total, 48-month term, renewal rate, and any included first-year extras on the same card.

2. Continue to checkout without assuming the card is your final bill. Hostinger's page says to choose a plan and complete checkout. Confirm whether the code is applied, the full amount due now, the currency, tax, and any added products. We did not perform this checkout.

3. For an existing subscription, check the actual renewal term. Hostinger says renewal periods and prices are shown during the renewal process. Check your account's selected term and total charge before renewing or leaving auto-renewal enabled. The public card's per-month renewal rate alone does not settle that total.

What this comparison can and cannot answer

It answers what the cited official cards displayed for an initial 48-month purchase on the check date. It also shows the published renewal rate for the same named plan. It does not prove a code will work for every account, that the offer will still be available later, or that a future renewal uses the same 48-month duration. No savings amount or percentage is calculated from a different plan, term, or unconfirmed renewal invoice.

FAQ

Is $2.84 a monthly Premium bill?

Not for the cited offer. The public card pairs $2.84/mo with $136.32 paid for 48 months, and Hostinger says the total is paid upfront.

Can I multiply the published renewal rate by 48 to get my next bill?

No. The public card does not select your next renewal duration or state your next invoice total. Hostinger says available renewal durations can change and the selected period is paid upfront. Check the duration and total shown for your account during renewal.

When does COUPONSPAGE expire?

The cited public coupon cards did not give an exact end date when checked September 25, 2026. This page does not assign one. Confirm the offer on Hostinger's current page and at checkout.

Can an existing customer use this code at renewal?

The public FAQ discusses discount codes for existing customers ordering new hosting plans and separately points to renewal deals in hPanel. It does not verify this code for an existing plan's renewal.
