WP Engine vs Kinsta: what will renewal cost?

A $31 monthly saving can disappear with usage. WP Engine lists $330/month for an existing Scale subscription's next renewal; Kinsta Lead lists $299/month. These are different plans, not equivalent quotes.

For 20 sites, compare your renewal and required capacity, the billing meter, and migration payback before switching.

Names first: WordPress.org supplies free software; WordPress.com sells a hosted service. WP Engine and Kinsta are separate managed hosts. Free software still needs hosting. See the distinction.

Official public pages read October 1, 2026. USD before tax. No customer invoice or checkout was verified for this comparison. Calculations below are labeled examples, not observed bills or performance tests.

A 20-site Lead example you can recalculate

Assumed month: 300 GB billable server bandwidth, 75 GB live storage throughout the month, and 1,100 GB billable CDN traffic. Monthly Lead subscription; no other extras.

$299 + (300 − 275) × $0.50 + (75 − 60) × $2 + (1,100 − 1,000) × $0.05 = $346.50 before tax.

A 20 GB storage add-on costs $20/month. If active for this entire month, storage allowance becomes 80 GB and the same scenario costs $336.50: $299 + $12.50 + $20 + $5.

This is an illustration using the published rates above. The add-on saves $10 under these assumptions. Daily storage changes or partial-month activation change the result. Compare $336.50 with your complete WP Engine bill; the $31 difference between base prices has already disappeared even before migration.

How do twenty sites and their staging copies count?

WP Engine's plan guide includes Production, Staging and Development within a site. Extra sites and storage can be separate purchases; included environments can still contribute billable visits under its counting rules.

Kinsta's staging documentation excludes staging disk from the plan's storage allocation. Premium Staging CDN usage still counts toward CDN consumption. Standard staging has lower resources and should not stand in for production capacity. Check legacy plans separately.

The Premium Staging add-on is $20 per environment/month, prorated. One optional premium environment is not the same purchase as another production site. Inventory production sites, included staging and paid environments in separate rows before adding up an agency quote.

Does “not live” mean “not billable”?

No single rule covers every host or meter. WP Engine's storage report includes non-production copies even when an environment is not another paid site. That can put pressure on the plan's storage allowance; it does not establish an automatic per-GB charge or a separate staging fee on your contract. Inspect the environment breakdown and the invoice line before changing plans.

Illustration, not an invoice: if live files and databases total 20 GB and a staging clone retains another 20 GB, those two copies contribute 40 GB to WP Engine account storage. Under Kinsta's published staging exclusion, the staging copy contributes zero to its plan storage total. Cloudways Flexible must still have disk for both copies, and SiteGround's free-space and inode conditions still apply. Backups, logs, caches and other environments make this an incomplete capacity model.

Use your last full billing cycles to price the same workload at each host. Keep CDN, storage, staging features, backup retention and staff time in separate lines. If the complete destination bill is not lower, moving has no savings-based payback; use the migration calculation before committing.

Why might a WordPress or plugin update appear later?

StageWhat to check at WP Engine
Core version rolloutMajor releases undergo platform testing before rollout. A major update can be deferred 30 days after it becomes available on the platform. Minor security/maintenance releases cannot be deferred and follow a shorter testing process. Core policy.
Finding an available versionWordPress.org and the WP Engine mirror are selectable update sources, with account and site settings. Failover can override a choice; WP-CLI continues using the mirror. A repository delay is a different question from core-release testing. Update-source guide.
Installing and checking pluginsSmart Plugin Manager separately schedules plugin/theme updates and uses visual checks and rollback. Check its schedule and results; a visual check does not detect every possible failure. Manager documentation.

These distinctions can explain a delay without diagnosing a particular forum report. They do not establish that every delayed plugin has been held for compatibility testing.

For the comparison budget, Kinsta Automatic Updates adds visual testing and rollback for $3 per enabled environment/month after its free first month: 20 enabled environments cost $60/month, or 40 cost $120/month. Native WordPress auto-updates are a different option. WP Engine's plan table lists its plugin/theme manager as optional on Essential and one licence included on Core/Enterprise; obtain the price and licence coverage needed for your portfolio.

One-time move cost = migration service + staff hours × hourly cost + overlapping subscriptions + other necessary setup.

Payback in months = one-time move cost ÷ positive monthly savings after usage, required features and administration.

Illustration: 6 hours × $50 + $100 overlap = $400. At a sustained $31/month saving, recovery takes about 12.9 months. These labour and overlap inputs are assumptions. If the new total costs more, this saving-based payback does not exist.

Quick answers before choosing

Is Kinsta always cheaper than WP Engine?

No. Choose a suitable plan and count usage, backup needs, paid environments and labour. Lead's lower listed base does not make it an equivalent replacement for every Scale contract. Agency 20 starts above the listed $330 Scale renewal before any extras.

Is managed hosting worth a 100% premium?

Price the workload and included responsibilities first. If the actual premium were $100/month and your team's time cost $50/hour, saving two hours of administration would offset it. That is a decision formula, not an observed performance or time-saving result for either host.

How should I compare WP Engine vs Kinsta pricing?

Use the same currency and service period, then add usage and required features. The plan table separates an existing renewal notice from current list prices and annual prepayment from monthly charges. Match capacity to your portfolio before treating two prices as alternatives.

What is my WP Engine renewal cost?

Your written renewal notice and contract establish the payable amount and covered term. The published schedule above is a reference for its named subscriptions; it does not determine every account's tax, add-ons or usage charges. See the invoice investigation steps.

Can a coupon fix an existing renewal increase?

Only terms covering that renewal can establish eligibility. Compare the written renewal notice with a retained-customer quote and the full migration alternative. Use our WP Engine bill investigation guide to identify the increased line first.
