Do I have to phone to cancel, and will I get a refund?

The official instructions provide online routes; no telephone step is specified. The following steps are paraphrases of the named official guides. We did not cancel an account.

  1. Shared hosting: Hosting List → Manage → disable auto-renew to let it expire later. For immediate cancellation, choose Cancel service, review the warning and confirm. Back up first: immediate cancellation erases hosted data. The domain registration continues separately.
  2. Dedicated server: the same guide directs you to Billing/Sales through Live Chat or email.
  3. Private Email: Private Email → Manage → Cancel → Cancel subscription. Its guide explicitly says Private Email subscriptions canceled through the Admin Panel are non-refundable.
