The first-year calculation

3 × $2 + 9 × $6 = $60. This is our arithmetic using the two displayed rates, and it matches the displayed subtotal. Dividing $60 by 12 gives a $5/month first-year average before taxes. The average is a calculation, not a separately offered monthly plan.

What happens after three months, and after one year?

Does cancelling an IONOS subscription require a phone call?

The official help page provides both routes. Its account instructions explain how to select a contract in the subscriptions area, choose cancellation of the entire contract, supply the requested reason and date details, and confirm the consequences after backing up data. That supports an online route in the published instructions. It does not prove that every region, legacy contract or account follows an identical flow. We did not sign in, disable renewal or cancel a live service.
