CLOUDWAYS OFFICIAL PROMO CHECK

Cloudways coupon code: does the official promo page list one?

Short answer: Yes. When HostDealRadar checked Cloudways’ official promo page on September 19, 2026, it displayed code SUMMER404. Cloudways says the code is applied automatically at checkout and gives 40% off all hosting plans for four months.

Scope and date

HostDealRadar checked Cloudways’ official US promo page on September 19, 2026. This is a source check, not a checkout test. Offers, eligibility, plan availability, taxes, and final totals can change before purchase.

The official answer

Cloudways’ promo page says it currently offers promo codes for eligible new customers. Its linked signup route contains coupon=SUMMER404, and the page says the code is already applied. Cloudways also says a user can apply only one discount code at checkout.

What Cloudways says about eligibility and later billing

Cloudways says this offer is for new users who upgrade from the free trial to a paid plan. Its FAQ says promo codes usually cannot be combined with other cloud discount codes, and only one discount code can be used at checkout. Cloudways also says the discount typically applies to the initial promotional period of a first-time purchase; after that period, standard pricing applies. A separate renewal promotion may occasionally be offered.

Cloudways sells hosted services. Its promo page does not describe physical shipping, and this guide does not claim a shipping threshold or a return policy.

Before you start a paid plan

  1. Open Cloudways’ official promo page and follow its official signup route; do not rely on a third-party coupon listing.
  2. Confirm that the code appears in the signup flow and that your account is eligible as a new user upgrading from the free trial.
  3. Review the plan, cloud provider, usage-based charges, add-ons, tax, and the total shown before payment.
  4. Confirm when the four-month introductory period ends and what standard pricing applies after it. A promotional rate is not a renewal-price guarantee.

What HostDealRadar does not claim: this guide does not establish that the offer will apply to every account or checkout, does not publish a separate renewal code, and does not claim an expiry date that Cloudways’ page did not state in the content checked.
