What this record proves — and does not prove

The stored source evidence covers the official response and its countdown statement. A previous edition of this guide called September 15, 2026 the offer end date. That date is not supported by a retained source excerpt in this release, so it is not presented here as a verified deadline. The September 16 countdown target and the zero counter are reported separately rather than treated as a confirmed time-zone conversion.

The official answer

The stored countdown evidence shows a September 16, 2026 target with all displayed units at zero. We therefore retain the material as historical and do not place it among current offers. This is a page check, not a checkout test or a check of every Cloudways product.

Read down that column and two things are visible that no single year shows on its own. The headline offer grew — 30% for 3 months, then 40% for 3 months, then 40% for 4 months, then 40% for 4 months with migrations added — and then fell back to 30% for 3 months in the August 2022 page. And the code naming never settled: 2018 and 2021 used the year, 2019 and 2022 used numbers, so 2019 is BFCM40 and not BFCM2019. Anyone reconstructing a missing year by pattern would get it wrong.

Two codes in that table carry a caveat about what our own table can prove.

BFCM18 is printed in two separate Cloudways posts from 2018, and both say 30% for 3 months — but only one of the two says the code was for a new account, so that one limit rests on a single source. BFCM40 is harder: in the same 2019 post, Cloudways also lists a partner’s unrelated plugin deal using the code BFCM40. The two lines sit far apart in one long article. The 40%/3-month reading above comes from the Cloudways sentence about its own hosting; the code string alone does not carry Cloudways’ identity.

The codes that only look like Cloudways codes

Search any coupon term built on this brand and a large share of what comes back is not a Cloudways discount at all. In Cloudways’ own 2018 round-up, other companies’ deals are listed under codes that contain the word cloudways — CloudWaysFriday for a WordPress theme shop, CLOUDWAYS-20 for a cart-migration service, cloudways-BFCM for a Magento extension vendor, and several more. They appear only inside each company’s own entry, one line at a time. Nothing on the page puts them next to Cloudways, which is why they circulate as Cloudways codes. The same thing happens to BFCM18: at least three other software vendors ran that exact code in the same week.

What the 2021 and 2022 promo pages did in their own markup

Four details below are only in the page source. A reader looking at the rendered page — or anything that reads the page as text — cannot see them at all.

  1. The coupon counter reads Promo Code 0000 in the markup, next to 300 Total Coupons. The four zeros are an unfilled template placeholder; the rendered page shows only the 300.
  2. The countdown digits — 30 Days, 18 Hours, 54 Mins, 09 Secs — are identical in the 2021-11-29, 2022-08-10 and 2022-12-03 snapshots. The same numbers were still on the page thirteen months later, so they are text, not a live clock. (The current page still carries a countdown; its state is the subject of the section above, not of this archive.)
  3. Both 2022 snapshots still carry the date-picker defaults Oct 27, 2021 00:00:01 and Nov 29, 2021 18:59:59 in the markup.
  4. On the 2022-12-03 page the FAQ and the terms block answer the same question differently. The FAQ section — the heading is “Does this offer apply to existing customers?” — says the offer “applies to all users who sign up for Cloudways and upgrade their accounts from trial to full”. Three screens down, the terms block says “This offer is only valid for new customers.” Both sentences are in the same file.

That last pair is worth keeping for a different reason. When Google’s AI Overview for cloudways coupon code was captured on 2026-09-21, its “Eligibility” line read “Valid only for new users upgrading from a free trial to a paid plan,” attributed to Cloudways. That is the narrower of the two sentences above, presented as the only one. The company’s own page from four years earlier did not agree with itself, and the summary picked a side.

The address on the old pages

Cloudways’ own page footers moved during this window. The 2018 and 2019 snapshots end with 52 Springvale, Pope Pius XII Street, Mosta MST2653, Malta; the 2021 and 2022 snapshots end with Junction Business Centre, 1st Floor Sqaq Lourdes, St Julians STJ3334, Malta. The change can be bracketed between those snapshots and no more precisely than that.

One page in the current results writes the company address as Junction Business Centre, 1st Floor Sqaq Lourdes, St Julians, Mosta STJ 3334, Malta — the street and postcode from the later footer, the town from the earlier one. We record the two archived versions and that page’s version side by side and leave the reading to you.

How this archive was checked

On 2026-09-21 we captured the top ten Google results for cloudways coupon code in a US-only, English-only session and read each one. Nine opened. One — a Reddit thread — returned a network-security block asking us to log in, so we have one result we could not read and are not counting it as a miss. Across the nine pages that did open, none of the following strings appears: BFCM18, BFCM2020, BFCM2021, the 2019 deadline “4th December”, 300 Total Coupons, the 30 Days 18 Hours 54 Mins 09 Secs countdown, or the old postcode MST2653.

Two strings do appear, and we are not claiming them. BFCM4030 and SUMMER306 are both listed on a third-party page in that top ten, each marked expired there. So the 2022 code is not unique to this record, and only the 2018 and 2019 rows plus the source-level findings above are.

One limit applies to the whole archive: for 2020 we have no offer text. Anyone stating what Cloudways’ 2020 offer was is stating something this evidence does not contain.

Before you start a paid plan

  1. Check the official promo and pricing pages, then confirm the actual total and any discount at checkout.
  2. Check whether your account qualifies for any offer shown in the order flow.
  3. Review the plan, cloud provider, usage charges, add-ons, tax, and total before paying.
  4. Do not infer a renewal price or a current promotion from historical page material.

Scope: this guide records the cited official source response only. It does not establish that no account-specific or separately advertised offer exists, or that any offer will apply to every checkout.
