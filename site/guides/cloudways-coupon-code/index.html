CLOUDWAYS OFFICIAL PROMO CHECK

Cloudways coupon code: what the official promo page currently shows

Short answer: On October 4, 2026, Cloudways’ official promo page displayed 40% off all hosting plans for four months and said the promo code was already applied. It did not show a code to enter or an exact end date. This is a dated page observation, not a checkout test; confirm the offer and total in your own signup flow.

Looking for an older code? The Cloudways coupon archive lists what Cloudways’ own pages printed from 2018 to 2022, with each saved response cited. Those records do not establish that an old code works today.

What this page check proves, and what it does not

The rendered official page was read on October 4, 2026 at 8:59 p.m. Pacific. It displayed the offer below, but no human-readable code or calendar deadline. The page said the code was applied automatically; we did not test signup, checkout, eligibility, or a final bill. The earlier September 15, 2026 end date was not supported by a retained source excerpt. The current deadline is unknown.

The official answer

Cloudways’ official promo page displayed a 40% discount on all hosting plans for four months, plus unlimited free migrations for WordPress and WooCommerce websites. Its FAQ says the offer is for new users who sign up and upgrade from the free trial to a paid plan, and that existing customers are not eligible. The page also says the promo code is applied automatically, but does not print a code visitors can type.

The page labels this a limited-time Summer offer but gives no exact calendar deadline in the visible offer. Its displayed starting prices were $11 to $6.60 per month for Cloudways Flexible and $99 to $59.40 per month for Cloudways Autonomous. These are the amounts shown on the promo page; they do not establish a post-promotion or renewal price.

Before you start a paid plan

1. Check the current official pages. Open the official promo and pricing pages, then confirm the actual total and any discount at checkout.

2. Check eligibility. Confirm that your account qualifies for the offer shown in the order flow. The saved December 2022 page contains conflicting eligibility statements for BFCM4030; neither proves an existing paid account qualified.

3. Check the whole bill. Review the plan, cloud provider, usage charges, add-ons, tax, and total before paying.

4. Keep historical evidence in the past. Do not infer a renewal price or a current promotion from archived page material. The December 2022 one-coupon terms describe that saved offer, not a current checkout rule.

Scope: this guide records the rendered official page as read on October 4 and distinguishes it from a separate saved response. It does not establish that the promotion will apply to every account or checkout.
