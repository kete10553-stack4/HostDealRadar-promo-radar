Cloudways coupon code: has the official offer expired?

Updated October 16, 2026; originally published September 19, 2026.

Short answer: Cloudways’ official promo page lists 40% off for four months, but its displayed September 16, 2026 end target has passed, so the page does not confirm the offer is still available.

Looking for an older code? The Cloudways coupon archive lists what Cloudways’ own pages printed from 2018 to 2022, with each saved response cited. Those records do not establish that an old code works today.

What this page check proves, and what it does not

The current official page was re-read on October 5, 2026 at 1:19 a.m. Pacific. Its countdown target is Sept 16, 2026 14:00:00, with no time zone stated. At the read time, the page DOM showed negative day, hour, minute, and second values because the target had passed; it was not at zero. The page’s signup links carry coupon=SUMMER404. The official answer

Cloudways’ official promo page displayed a 40% discount on all hosting plans for four months, plus unlimited free migrations for WordPress and WooCommerce websites. Its FAQ says the offer is for new users who sign up and upgrade from the free trial to a paid plan, and that existing customers are not eligible. The page also says the promo code is applied automatically, but does not print a code visitors can type.

The page embeds the exact countdown target Sept 16, 2026 14:00:00, but does not state a time zone. When read on October 5, 2026 at 1:19 a.m. Pacific, its countdown values had gone below zero. Its displayed starting prices were $11 to $6.60 per month for Cloudways Flexible and $99 to $59.40 per month for Cloudways Autonomous. These are the amounts shown on the promo page; they do not establish a post-promotion or renewal price.

Before you start a paid plan

1. Check the current official pages. Open the official promo and pricing pages, then confirm the actual total and any discount at checkout.

2. Check eligibility. Confirm that your account qualifies for the offer shown in the order flow. The saved December 2022 page contains conflicting eligibility statements for BFCM4030; neither proves an existing paid account qualified.

3. Check the whole bill. Review the plan, cloud provider, usage charges, add-ons, tax, and total before paying.

4. Keep historical evidence in the past. Do not infer a renewal price or a current promotion from archived page material. The December 2022 one-coupon terms describe that saved offer, not a current checkout rule.

How Cloudways bills for usage

Cloudways describes Flexible as monthly pay-as-you-go billing based on server usage. Its help guide describes Autonomous billing by the hours an app is active. The public pricing FAQ says changes to a hosting plan are reflected on the invoice as they happen during the month. These are different billing descriptions, so compare the named product and selected server configuration, not just a monthly price label.

Cloudways says it invoices for the previous month’s usage at the start of the next month. Its pricing page says the first week, while its billing FAQ gives a window from the 1st through the 6th. The help guide says a month-to-date estimate can differ from the final invoice by up to 3%. Add-ons, usage beyond included limits, and taxes based on billing location may also affect the bill.

The pages checked here do not establish an annual prepaid total for the configurations described by third-party coupon pages. A displayed monthly equivalent is not enough to establish the actual charge, promotion eligibility, or discount coverage. Review the selected configuration and invoice details in your account before paying.
