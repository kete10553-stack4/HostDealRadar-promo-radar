Can a Hostinger domain renewal coupon code reduce the next bill?

We did not verify one in the official sources linked here. A first-year domain price or a free-domain bundle does not establish a renewal discount. Use the separately labeled renewal price for your extension, then check the renewal order for your account. This finding does not rule out an account-specific offer.

Moving a domain instead of registering one?

The free-domain page also offers an eligible domain transfer with a new Premium or Unlimited plan of at least 12 months. What to check before you pay

  1. Choose your route: new registration, transfer, or renewal. Search for the exact name and extension on Hostinger's domain page.
  2. Check the selected registration period and the complete payable amount. Keep the first-year price separate from later charges and fees.
  3. If you have a coupon code for a Hostinger domain, check its product and term restrictions and whether the official order accepts it. Do not treat a hosting-code card as proof.
  4. Read the relevant refund exception above before submitting payment. Save the order terms you actually receive.

The official domain-search page includes privacy protection only for supported extensions. Do not assume it applies to every ending. Official domain-search page, checked September 29, 2026; record D5.
