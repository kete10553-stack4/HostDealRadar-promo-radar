Privacy

This static site does not require accounts or collect purchase details.

Provider links open their own sites, where their privacy policies apply. We do not use affiliate-cookie injection or sell visitor information.

HostDealRadar currently does not display third-party advertising or use affiliate links. If either is introduced, we will disclose it here and update this page.

Optional Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand page visits and engagement and improve this website. The Google Analytics tag loads only after you choose Allow analytics. Reject analytics keeps that tag from loading. Google processes usage data on our behalf, including page paths, browser and device information and approximate location. We do not deliberately send your name, email address, purchase details, or page URL query strings to Google Analytics.

After you allow analytics, Google Analytics may store first-party cookies named _ga and _ga_<identifier> in your browser. We configure their lifetime to 180 days; browsers may shorten this. Advertising consent remains denied, and our tag disables Google signals and advertising personalization.

We store your analytics choice and its date in your browser's local storage for up to 180 days. This stores the choice without sending it to Google before consent. You can change or withdraw your choice through Analytics cookie settings in the footer of any page. Rejection stops subsequent Analytics collection on this page and removes accessible Google Analytics cookies for this hostname; it does not erase data already sent. If browser storage is unavailable, we ask again on the next page.

Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services, the Google Privacy Policy, and the Google data processing terms. Google and its contracted processors may process data in other countries. You may also use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Hosting and traffic statistics

Cloudflare delivers this site and processes network requests, including IP addresses, for hosting and security. Its server-side traffic statistics operate separately from the optional Google Analytics tag. Rejecting Google Analytics does not stop the processing needed to deliver and protect the website. Read the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
